# Malwa Associates — Vehicle & Home Loans + Insurance Landing Page

A fast, responsive, and conversion-optimized business landing page built for **Malwa Associates (Bathinda, Punjab)**. The website highlights 25+ years of trusted financial matchmaking services across vehicle loans, heavy machinery finance, home loans, and insurance plans.

---

## 🚀 Features

- **Hero Background Carousel:** Smooth auto-fading background slider showcasing Car, Tractor, JCB, and Home Loan imagery with a dark high-contrast overlay.
- **Detailed Flagship Service Cards:** Dedicated dark-themed cards highlighting features, key metrics, and bulleted details for:
  - Car Loans (New & Used)
  - Tractor Loans (Kisan-friendly seasonal repayments)
  - JCB & Commercial Heavy Machinery Finance
  - Home Loans (Purchase, construction & balance transfer)
  - General & Life Insurance (Motor, health & life coverage)
- **Continuous Partner Marquee:** Smooth, infinite left-to-right scrolling ribbon showcasing trusted financial partners and NBFCs with both logo and brand name:
  - AU Small Finance Bank
  - Tata Capital
  - Hero FinCorp
  - Bajaj Finserv
  - IDFC FIRST Bank
  - Cholamandalam (Chola)
- **Interactive Lead Capture Form:** Client-side validation for Indian 10-digit mobile numbers with direct dispatch to WhatsApp via pre-formatted messages.
- **Google Maps Integration:** Direct office location redirect link connected to Google Maps (`Bathinda, Punjab`).
- **Floating Quick-Action Buttons:** Persistent Call and WhatsApp quick-access floating buttons pinned to the bottom-right corner for mobile-first user experience.
- **Modern Design Tokens & Performance:** Lightweight pure CSS3 (flexbox, CSS grid, CSS custom variables) and vanilla JavaScript with zero third-party framework overhead.
